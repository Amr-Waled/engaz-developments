# SEO, conversion, and measurement

## Technical SEO

- Give each indexable page one descriptive title, one meta description, one canonical URL, and one H1.
- Link important pages through crawlable navigation or contextual links.
- Keep sitemap entries accurate and exclude utility or duplicate URLs.
- Use structured data only for facts visible or verifiable on the site.
- Provide descriptive alt text for meaningful images and empty alt text for decorative ones.
- Optimize the largest above-the-fold image, preload only critical resources, and avoid layout shifts by declaring dimensions.
- Validate robots rules, status codes, mobile rendering, social preview tags, and redirects on production.

## Search content

Map one search intent to one useful page. Build content around customer decisions, locations, project types, proof, FAQs, and process—not around repeated keyword variants. Keep changing information such as price and availability dated or routed to a live sales response.

## Conversion architecture

For each page define:

- Primary action and secondary action.
- Information needed before a visitor can act.
- Objections and the proof that resolves them.
- Minimum form data required for useful follow-up.
- Direct fallback such as phone or WhatsApp.

Use progressive commitment: browsing, filtering, project interest, short lead form, then deeper qualification. Preserve project, campaign, referrer, and UTM context in the CRM.

## Measurement

Track events with consistent names and parameters. At minimum measure project views, project filter usage, CTA clicks, WhatsApp, calls, social exits, form start, form errors, form success, and qualified-lead outcomes when CRM data allows it.

Measure conversion rate by source and landing page, lead-to-contact time, qualified-lead rate, appointment rate, and cost per qualified lead. Do not optimize only for raw submissions.

Respect consent, privacy, retention, and platform policies. Never place secrets or private tokens in client-side code.
